Grass005 is a short clean lawn. It replaced Grass001, which is wild meadow: broad
dock leaves and seed heads, which stretched across a football pitch read as weeds.

Modified from the originals: resized 2K to 1K; the colour map mean-normalised per
channel to mid grey so it modulates the pitch colour instead of replacing it, with
the ambient occlusion folded into it because the Standard shader samples its own
occlusion map at the main tiling and so cannot carry a detail-scale one; the
NormalGL map renormalised after the resize. The roughness and displacement maps
are unused. See Tools/grassprep.py.
