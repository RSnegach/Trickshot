  qwantani_sunset_puresky

Modified from the originals: tone-mapped from linear HDR to 8-bit sRGB and saved
as JPEG. See Tools/skyprep.py for the exact curve and for why (the game renders in
gamma colour space with no post-processing stack, so HDR range would only clip).

Added later, with DIFFERENT provenance - worth stating plainly rather than filing
them alongside the five above as if they matched:
  qwantani_mid_morning_puresky
  kloppenheim_06_puresky
  qwantani_puresky

These two were NOT tone-mapped here. They are Poly Haven's own 8192x4096 tone-mapped
JPEG exports, downscaled to 4096x2048 (Lanczos) and re-encoded at JPEG q90 to match
the size of the rest of the folder. So their tone curve is Poly Haven's, not
Tools/skyprep.py's, and the two sets are not guaranteed to grade identically.
See Tools/skyfetch.py, which also measures each candidate over the band the menu
camera can actually see (elevation 0 to +8 degrees) - that measurement, not overall
image quality, is what these were chosen on.
