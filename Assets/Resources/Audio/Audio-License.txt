Modified from the original: vari-speeded down 2.2x so the modal cluster and ring length
match a full size aluminium crossbar rather than a hand sized pipe, mixed with a
synthesized 80 Hz thump for the ball's own contribution, high-passed at 45 Hz, and
written out as 44.1 kHz mono 16-bit. See Tools/postprep.py for the exact chain and for
why each step is there.
